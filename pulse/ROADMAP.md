# Pulse product goal

Pulse is Aiden's private, iPhone-first signal layer. It checks sources every 15 minutes, stores everything it detects, and interrupts only when a signal is useful, urgent, unusual, or personally relevant.

## product surface

- phone-only, dark, minimal, fast to scan
- three clear areas: signals, controls, and connections
- no dashboard clutter, generic chat assistant, Discord bot, or life-OS surface
- every notification opens the exact event page with source evidence, why it mattered, and safe actions
- suppressed events remain searchable in history and explainable in the audit view

## personal coverage

Pulse should prioritize meaningful changes involving:

- La Ceja weather, local impact, earthquakes, school, travel, packages, purchases, security, and important people
- Apple, iOS betas and confirmed software news, ChatGPT/OpenAI, Discord, Instagram, GitHub/projects, and service outages
- Warzone/COD, Rocket League, Unstable SMP/Universe, Minecraft, and Roblox major updates
- connected Gmail, Google Calendar, Discord, Apple context, browser location, and AWS Luna when legitimately available

Source discovery is supplemental. Fixed official sources remain primary, all candidates use the same canonical event, clustering, trust, relevance, cooldown, quiet-hour, and push pipeline, and search results never push merely because they were found.

## completion bar

The goal is not complete until the remaining Pulse 0.2 work is either implemented and tested or recorded as a genuine external/platform blocker. Before calling it complete, verify production health, the 15-minute worker, real provider syncs, iPhone push/deep-link behavior, and the final phone UI on a fresh production load.
